## 💧 Current Projects

### **1️⃣ Nyamira Water Treatment Plants Project**
> Construction of 5 water treatment plants and a 177.2 km distribution network serving 12,651 households.  
**Budget:** KES 750 Million (USD 6 Million)

### **2️⃣ UNCDF Proposal — Decentralized Wastewater Treatment Facilities (DTFs)**
> A proposal to establish 8 DTFs in emerging urban centers within Nyamira County, aligned with **SDG 6** and **SDG 11**.

### **3️⃣ Ltome–Katip Indigenous AI Project**
> Indigenous-led AI and bioacoustics project between the **Samburu (Kenya)** and **Shuar (Ecuador)** communities, analyzing elephant and rat communication patterns using **AudioMoths**, **Arbimon**, and **machine learning**.
